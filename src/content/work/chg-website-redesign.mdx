Community Health Group is a nonprofit health plan serving 300,000+ members across San Diego County with Medicare Advantage and Medi-Cal coverage.

I led the redesign of the corporate website, from first prototype to the code that shipped on an enterprise CMS. I worked with the engineering team and owned the design throughout. I also set up Google Analytics for the site, so the impact could be measured.

<Callout label="Result">**+20% engagement** after launch, on a site that meets WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards and HIPAA requirements.</Callout>

## The challenge

A health plan website carries a lot: benefits, forms, notices and regulated content, for people who often arrive with a specific, urgent question. Many members are older adults or on Medi-Cal, so clarity and accessibility matter more than visual polish.

<Todo>What was not working on the old site. One or two concrete problems are enough, such as confusing navigation, content that was hard to find, or an analytics number that showed it.</Todo>

## Goals

- Help visitors find what they came for faster
- Meet WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards and HIPAA compliance
- Ship on the existing enterprise CMS so content teams can keep publishing on their own
- Set up Google Analytics so the redesign could be measured against real behavior
