GoShare is a last-mile and middle-mile logistics company with 100k customers and 20k delivery professionals. I led design for the product and worked with project managers, engineering, marketing and the business side.

<Callout label="Result">The multi-stop booking work I designed increased bookings by **25%**.</Callout>

## What I looked at

The "Additional info" field on each delivery location is a free-text box. Customers use it for things like parking, a contact, or the building. For example: "Gate code is 4912."

<Todo>Screenshot of the original Additional info field.</Todo>

I sampled 93 customer entries to see what people were actually writing.

| Share | Entries | What customers wrote |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 33.3% | 40 | Outdoor number or entry code |
| 18.3% | 22 | Contact information |
| 15.0% | 18 | Stairs or elevator |
| 10.8% | 13 | Parking availability |

Most of the field was being used to answer the same few questions, and a text box gave customers no hint of which ones mattered.

## What I changed

I turned those recurring answers into direct questions and built the booking flow as a short survey. Many customers use the app only once or twice a year, so a guided flow helps people who don't remember how it works.
